Limit Bar 1.0.7

A rebuilt Settings window that fits its content and makes alert thresholds quick to adjust.

Codex Claude Code Antigravity
Download 1.0.7 View on GitHub

What’s new

  • Settings now sizes itself to its content, so there is no empty space below the footer, and it resizes smoothly when notifications are switched on or off.
  • Alert thresholds were rebuilt. Each value sits in a single card you can type into or nudge with a stepper, colour coded from early to critical.
  • A new scale under the thresholds shows where each alert falls as a balance drains from full to empty.
  • Providers, general options, and notifications are now grouped under clear section headings.
  • A connected provider shows its account or plan instead of repeating the word Connected.
  • Fixed the developer credit in the Settings footer being invisible in Light Appearance.

Requires macOS 15.6 or later. Limit Bar reads local provider sessions and does not require browser cookies.
